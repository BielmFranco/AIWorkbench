# Worked example: denied connection

## Supplied observation
In the provided notes, two users abandon workspace connection after denying permission because the page returns to the start with no explanation. These are supplied observations, not a new study.

## Hypothesis and design
Preserving the selected workspace and explaining how to retry may reduce confusion. Add a denied state with a reason, the retained selection, a retry path and an alternative if access cannot be granted.

## Validation plan
Ask representative participants to connect a workspace, intentionally encounter denial, and recover. Observe whether they identify the correct account and understand the permission. Do not claim reduced abandonment before measuring it.
