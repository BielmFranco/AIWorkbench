# Retrieval contract and evidence

## Corpus lifecycle
Source IDs/revisions, access rules, freshness, deletion, cache invalidation and owner.

## Retrieval decision
Query tasks, chunking rationale, lexical/dense baseline, chosen cutoff and labeled passages.

| Question | Authorized/current evidence | Retrieved IDs | Answer/citation check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| User task | Relevant passages or none | Actual retrieval | Support or abstention |

## Metrics and limits
Retrieval ranking/coverage and answer quality separately, permission/deletion checks, settings and unexecuted tests.
